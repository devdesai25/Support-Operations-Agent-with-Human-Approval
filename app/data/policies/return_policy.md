# Return & Exchange Policy

## 1. Return Window
Customers may initiate a return within **14 calendar days** from the date the package is marked as delivered by the carrier. Return requests submitted after 14 calendar days cannot be accepted.

## 2. Item Condition Requirements
To qualify for a full refund or exchange:
- The sneakers or apparel must be **completely unworn, unwashed, and undamaged**.
- The original shoe box, manufacturer tags, extra laces, and accessories must be intact and in pristine condition.
- Sneakers returned with creased leather, scuffed outsoles, or missing shoe trees will be rejected and sent back at the customer's expense.

## 3. Return Shipping & Restocking
- A prepaid return shipping label will be provided once a return is authorized.
- A **$10.00 restocking fee** applies to standard returns and is automatically deducted from the refund balance.
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or won via exclusive limited-edition "Raffle Drops" are non-returnable unless defective.
