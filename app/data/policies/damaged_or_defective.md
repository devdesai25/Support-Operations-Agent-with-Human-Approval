# Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

## 1. Reporting Window
Any claim for an item that is damaged in transit, defective from the manufacturer, or materially different from the ordered product must be reported within **48 hours of carrier delivery**.

## 2. Documentation Required
Customers must submit:
- High-resolution clear photos showing the defect, tear, stitching flaw, or broken box.
- A photo of the shipping box label showing the tracking number and recipient address.
- A photo of the inner size tag / SKU barcode.

## 3. Resolution
Upon verification by our support operations team:
- If a replacement is available in the exact SKU and size, a replacement order is dispatched immediately.
- If out of stock, a **100% full refund** (including all original shipping charges) is approved and issued.
