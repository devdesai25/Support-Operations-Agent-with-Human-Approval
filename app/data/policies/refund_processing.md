# Refund Processing & Payment Reversals

## 1. Approval & Verification
All refund requests require operational review and explicit confirmation before funds are disbursed. 
- Refunds for damaged or defective items require photographic evidence.
- Refunds for cancellations must adhere to the order cancellation cutoff window.

## 2. Payout Timelines & Methods
- Once a refund is executed, funds are returned strictly to the **original payment method** (credit card, Apple Pay, or PayPal).
- Banking institutions typically take **3 to 5 business days** to credit the customer's account statement.
- Under no circumstances can refunds be disbursed in cash, third-party wire transfers, or to an alternate payment method.

## 3. Partial vs Full Refunds
- Partial refunds may be issued if an order contains multiple items and only a subset is returned or unavailable.
- Shipping fees from the original order are non-refundable unless the return was necessitated by our warehouse error.
