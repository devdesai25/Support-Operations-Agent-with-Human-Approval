# Anti-Fraud, Bot Prevention & Fair Play Policy

## 1. Automated Purchase Tools & Bots
The use of scripts, automated form fillers, or third-party bots during drop releases or checkout is strictly prohibited. 
- Suspicious activity triggers automatic security holds on orders.
- Orders identified as automated or multi-instance checkout will be cancelled immediately without advance warning.

## 2. Quantity Limits
Unless explicitly stated otherwise, individual customers are restricted to a maximum of **one pair per sneaker style** across all colorways during hype launch windows. Multiple orders shipping to the identical freight forwarding hub or billing card will be consolidated or cancelled.
