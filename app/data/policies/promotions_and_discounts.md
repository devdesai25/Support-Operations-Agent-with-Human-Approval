# Promotional Codes & Discount Terms

## 1. Applicability & Restrictions
- Promotional discount codes must be entered prior to order completion at checkout. Discounts cannot be applied retroactively to already submitted or fulfilled orders.
- Only **one discount code** can be applied per checkout session.
- Exclusions: Limited-edition raffle drops, quickstrike releases, gift cards, and select collaboration apparel are strictly excluded from coupon discounts.

## 2. Refund Calculation on Discounted Orders
- If an item purchased with a promotional code is returned or refunded, the refund will equal the **net discounted price actually paid**, not the original manufacturer retail price (MSRP).
