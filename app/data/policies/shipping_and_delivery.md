# Shipping & Delivery Guidelines

## 1. Domestic Shipping Tiers
- **Standard Ground Shipping**: Delivery within **3 to 5 business days**. Complimentary for orders over $150.00.
- **Express 2-Day Air**: Guaranteed delivery within **2 business days** when placed prior to 1:00 PM EST.
- **Overnight Priority**: Next-business-day delivery.

## 2. Signature Requirement & Lost Packages
- All orders totaling **$300.00 or higher** require a direct physical adult signature upon delivery to prevent package theft.
- If a tracking number indicates "Delivered" but the customer cannot locate the package, a trace inquiry must be filed with the carrier within 72 hours.
- We cannot re-route shipments once handed off to the logistics carrier.
